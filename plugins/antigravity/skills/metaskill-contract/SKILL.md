# MetaSkill Contract (`/metaskill-contract`)

1. Inspect the relevant workspace files read-only first.
2. Propose 2–5 requirements (`REQ-1`, `REQ-2`, ...) in `SPEC.md` under `## Contract`, each paired with a concrete check (`npm test`, `file: preview/<artifact>.png`, or `manual: ...`) and explicit `Out of scope`.
3. Validate with `mtsk contract validate` before editing code.
